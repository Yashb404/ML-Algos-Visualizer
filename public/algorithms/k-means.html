K-Means Clustering (Iris Dataset Simulation)

What is happening here?

K-Means alternates between assigning each point to its nearest centroid and updating centroids to the mean of assigned points. Repeat this for k = 2, 3, and 4 to compare how cluster compactness and separation change with the number of groups.
