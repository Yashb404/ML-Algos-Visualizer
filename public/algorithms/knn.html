K-Nearest Neighbors (KNN)

What is happening here?

KNN classifies a new sample by computing distances to all labeled points, selecting the K closest neighbors, and taking a majority vote. Smaller K values can capture local details but be noisy, while larger K values create smoother, more stable decision behavior.
